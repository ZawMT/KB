# Ecosystem - Frameworks and Gems

**Web frameworks:**
- **Rails** - the most popular full-stack Ruby web framework; "convention over configuration," MVC, includes ActiveRecord (ORM), routing, views, etc.
- **Sinatra** - minimal, unopinionated micro-framework; good for small apps or APIs, and often used to learn "what Rails does under the hood."
- **Hanami** - a newer, more modular full-stack framework, positioned as a lighter-weight alternative to Rails.
- **Roda** - very fast, small routing-tree-based framework.
- **Grape** - focused specifically on building APIs.

**Popular gems (libraries) regardless of framework:**
- **RSpec** - the dominant testing framework in the Ruby world (alternative to Ruby's built-in Minitest).
- **Devise** - authentication (login/signup) for Rails apps.
- **Sidekiq** - background job processing.
- **Puma** - the most common web server for running Rails/Rack apps.
- **Nokogiri** - HTML/XML parsing (like BeautifulSoup in Python).
- **Faraday/HTTParty** - HTTP client libraries.
- **Pry** - a much more powerful alternative to `irb` for debugging/REPL work.
