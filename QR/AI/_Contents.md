## AI
### Contents

#### [Back to main contents](/Contents.md)

AI (Artificial Intelligence) is the field of building computer systems that can perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence, such as 
- learning, 
- reasoning, 
- problem-solving, 
- planning,
- motor control,
- and understanding language. 
In short, it's about creating machines that can mimic cognitive functions to make decisions or predictions from data.
