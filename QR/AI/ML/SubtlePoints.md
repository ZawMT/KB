| DL&nbsp;vs&nbsp;NLP | AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL ⊃ LLM <br /> AI ⊃ ML ⊃ NLP ⊃ LLM <br /> NLP is the domain (focused on language tasks like translation, sentiment analysis, or chatbots).
